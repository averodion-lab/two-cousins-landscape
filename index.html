Frequently Asked Questions

What kinds of projects do you take on?

Hardscape of any type, pools, landscape and walls. That covers one patio or retaining wall up to a complete backyard.

Can you build on a steep or sloped yard?

Yes. The project in our video started as a cut-back hillside of red clay. Retaining walls and grading turned it into usable, level ground.

Do I need separate contractors for each part?

No. Stonework, walls, pool work and planting can all run as a single project with us.

How long will my project take?

It depends on the size of the job, access to the site and the weather. Ask for a timeline when you request your estimate.
